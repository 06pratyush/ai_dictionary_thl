Entropy

/ˈɛntrəpi/en·tro·pynoun
Information Theory CoreFoundational

Definition

1.[in information theory] a measure of the uncertainty or randomness inherent in a random variable or source of data.

The high entropy of the source indicated that the data was highly unpredictable.

Formal statement

H(X) = -sum(p(x_i) log_2 p(x_i))

The base-2 logarithm yields the unit of bits.

Etymology

From Greek entropein, meaning 'to transform' or 'to change', adopted into thermodynamics by Rudolf Clausius.
